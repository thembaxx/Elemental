Explore the elements.

Explore chemical elements with interactive 3D atom models, electron configurations, periodic property heatmaps and quizzes in Elemental.

What you can do

  • A 3D Bohr-style atom inspector with camera rotation and reset
  • Element search and a periodic-table drawer
  • Atomic mass, electron configurations and element summaries
  • Heatmaps for electronegativity, radius, ionization energy and atomic mass
  • A four-question chemistry quiz with explanations

Scope and privacy

  • The current dataset contains 23 selected elements, not detailed coverage of all 118.
  • Bohr-style rings are simplified teaching diagrams, not literal electron paths or quantum simulations.
  • Atomic-property values are educational reference data; use authoritative scientific sources for precision work.
  • The app is an interactive web experience that requires JavaScript; 3D rendering also needs WebGL support.
